FIELD

A video player built like a field recorder.

Putty keys over the picture, a tape counter for time, and a wheel you roll for volume. While you watch, all of it steps out of the way.

Roll the volume, press the key, turn your phone. Everything here is the real player.

Two things you’ll want to touch.

Make it yours with two choices.

Pick what the keys are made of and the one colour that marks where you are. Everything else stays the same.

Add it to your page.

  1. 1

    Install Video.js 10

    npm install @videojs/html @videojs/mux-video
  2. 2

    Add FIELD

    <link rel="stylesheet" href="field.css">
<script type="module" src="field.js"></script>
  3. 3

    Drop in your video

    <video-player>
  <media-container class="field" data-field-theme="putty">
    <mux-video src="your-video.m3u8" playsinline></mux-video>
    <!-- FIELD's keys: paste from field.html -->
  </media-container>
</video-player>
